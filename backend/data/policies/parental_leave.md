# Parental Leave Policy

## Global

- Parental leave supports employees welcoming a new child through birth or adoption.
- Employees should apply at least 30 days before the expected leave start date whenever possible.
- Supporting documentation may be requested by HR.

## India

- Eligible employees receive up to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave.
- Eligible fathers receive 15 days of paid paternity leave.
- Adoption leave is available in accordance with company policy.

## Germany

- Employees may take parental leave according to German labor regulations.
- Parents may be eligible for government-supported parental benefits.
- Employees should coordinate leave plans with HR before the expected start date.

## United States

- Eligible employees receive up to 12 weeks of parental leave, subject to company policy and applicable laws.
- Paid benefits depend on employment type and company policy.
- HR approval and supporting documentation are required before leave begins.
