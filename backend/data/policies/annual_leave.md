# Annual Leave Policy

## Global

- Annual leave is available to all full-time employees.
- Leave requests should be submitted at least 7 calendar days in advance whenever possible.
- All leave requests require manager approval before they are confirmed.
- Leave is subject to business requirements and team availability.
- Annual leave can only be applied for scheduled working days.
- Weekends (Saturday and Sunday) and company-recognized public holidays are not deducted from an employee's annual leave balance.
- If a leave request spans weekends or public holidays, only the applicable working days are counted as annual leave.

## India

- Employees receive 18 days of Annual Leave per calendar year.
- Leave is credited monthly throughout the year.
- Up to 10 unused days may be carried forward to the next year.
- Any balance exceeding the carry-forward limit expires at year-end.
- Consecutive leave exceeding 10 working days may require manager discussion.

## Germany

- Employees receive 30 days of paid annual leave each year.
- Leave is credited at the beginning of the calendar year.
- Annual leave cannot be carried forward unless required by applicable labor regulations.
- Employees are encouraged to schedule leave well in advance.
- Manager approval is required before travel arrangements are made.

## United States

- Employees receive 15 days of paid annual leave per year.
- Leave accrues throughout the year based on employment status.
- Carry forward is not permitted unless approved by HR.
- Requests should normally be submitted at least one week before the planned leave.
- Unused leave expires at the end of the calendar year.
