# Annual Leave Policy

## Global

- Annual leave must be approved by the manager.
- Leave requests should be submitted at least 7 days in advance.

## India

- Employees receive 18 days of annual leave.
- Maximum carry forward is 10 days.

## Germany

- Employees receive 30 days of annual leave.
- Carry forward is not allowed.

## United States

- Employees receive 15 days of annual leave.
