# Maternity Leave Policy

## Global

- Maternity leave is intended to support employees before and after childbirth.
- Employees should notify their manager and HR as early as reasonably possible.
- Supporting medical documentation may be required to confirm the expected delivery date.
- Maternity leave should be planned in coordination with HR and the employee's manager.

## India

- Eligible employees receive up to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave.
- Maternity leave may be taken before and after childbirth in accordance with applicable law.
- Employees should submit the expected leave dates and supporting medical documentation to HR.
- Additional leave may be available in cases covered by applicable maternity regulations.
- Employees should coordinate their return-to-work date with HR before the end of maternity leave.

## Germany

- Maternity protection and leave are provided in accordance with German maternity protection laws.
- Employees are generally protected from work during the statutory maternity protection period surrounding childbirth.
- Employees should notify HR of the expected delivery date as early as reasonably possible.
- Medical documentation may be required to establish the expected delivery date.
- Additional parental leave may be available after the maternity protection period under the applicable parental leave policy.

## United States

- Maternity leave is provided according to applicable federal, state, and company leave requirements.
- Eligible employees may receive job-protected leave under applicable laws and company policies.
- Paid benefits depend on employment status, applicable state requirements, and company policy.
- Employees should notify HR as early as possible and provide supporting documentation when required.
- Maternity leave may be coordinated with parental leave, medical leave, or other applicable leave programs.
