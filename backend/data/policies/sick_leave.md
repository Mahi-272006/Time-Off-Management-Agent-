# Sick Leave Policy

## Global

- Sick leave is intended for illness, injury, or medical treatment.
- Employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible.
- Medical documentation may be required for extended absences.
- Sick leave must not be used for annual vacation purposes.

## India

- Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave each calendar year.
- A medical certificate is required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive working days.
- Unused sick leave cannot be carried forward.
- Employees should inform their reporting manager on the first day of absence.
- HR may request additional medical documentation if necessary.

## Germany

- Employees are entitled to paid sick leave according to German labor laws.
- A medical certificate may be required from the fourth consecutive day of absence.
- Sick leave does not reduce annual leave entitlement.
- Employees should notify both their manager and HR as early as possible.
- Extended medical leave may require additional supporting documents.

## United States

- Employees receive 10 paid sick leave days per calendar year.
- Medical documentation may be requested for absences longer than three consecutive days.
- Unused sick leave expires at the end of the calendar year.
- Employees should notify their manager before the start of the workday whenever possible.
- Sick leave may be used for personal illness or approved medical appointments.
