# Sick Leave Policy

## Global

- Sick leave is intended for illness or medical treatment.
- Employees should notify their manager as soon as possible.
- Medical documentation may be required for extended absences.

## India

- Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year.
- A medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days.
- Unused sick leave cannot be carried forward.

## Germany

- Employees are entitled to paid sick leave according to German labor laws.
- A medical certificate may be required from the fourth consecutive day of absence.
- Sick leave does not reduce annual leave balance.

## United States

- Employees receive 10 paid sick leave days per year.
- Medical documentation may be requested for absences longer than 3 days.
- Unused sick leave expires at the end of the calendar year.
